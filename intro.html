Aamon: The Duke of Shards

Meet Aamon — one of the most feared heroes in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang.

Calm, elegant, and deadly, Aamon strikes from the shadows using razor-sharp crystal shards and the power to turn invisible, he confuses opponents and launches powerful surprise attacks. Aamon excels at quickly taking down weaker heroes and escaping safely after a fight, though requiring good timing, smart positioning, and confidence.
